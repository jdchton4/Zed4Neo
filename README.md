# Zed4Neo // jdchton4
## Intro 
* Zed4Neo is a vibe coded project (built with Gemini 4) I started over the course of a few weeks. It is a single file game written in javascript alongside three.js, HTML, and CSS. 
* The game itself is a top-down or third-person shooter (This depends on the camera mode specified, chosen with [Q]).
* All assets are synthesized either within the code itself, or use public asset libraries (for icons and fonts). This includes music, voice lines, 3D models, and UI

## How to Run
1. Download Zed4Neo.html
2. Run in any browser that supports running HTML files (Chrome, Edge, Firefox)
3. Click any button in the title screen to enter the game
4. Note: Some browsers struggle to run this game (Notably, some deprecated or experimental browser builds were found to struggle. Google Chrome Beta worked fine, but Google Chrome Canary struggled)

[Alternatively, Click this](https://jdchton4.github.io/zed3/Zed4Neo.html)

## Premise
Play as Zed3, an android construct completing tasks to 'clean up' failed experiments and rogue machines. Utilize Zed3's movement kit, upgradeable and modifiable weapon arsenal, ability kit, and any advantage you can find to complete each mission while avoiding level hazards and enemy threats.


## Content
* 9 Main Missions + 1 Final Mission

* 19 Upgradeable Weapons + 4 Tiers / Weapon + 13 Modifications / Weapon

## Tutorial
### Upgrades
* Increase player level by defeating enemies. Use rewarded *Spec Points* [SP] to increase player stats, and *Tech points* [TP] to unlock and upgrade abilities in the *Tech Matrix* menu
* Level completion also rewards *Spec points* and *Tech points*
* More points are awarded on 1st complete
* Abilities can be loaded into Zed3, with a limit of 4 active abilities and 4 passive ablities ***Without the use of augments***
* The specific slot for each ability is also chosen in the *Tech Matrix* Menu
* Defeated enemies and loot chests drop *credits* and *parts* - Use them to craft weapons, weapon attachments, and armor in the *Weapons Lab* and *Armor Foundry* Menu
* Weapons and Armor get stronger with use - increasing their prestige from Mk. I to Mk. VI
* Salvage unwanted weapons and armor for *Alloy Scrap*, used for speeding up the prestige process
* Defeated enemies can drop *Cores*. There are 2 types: *Augment Cores* and *Ascension Cores*
* Use *Augment Cores* to craft *Augments* that can be slotted into Zed3's Dorsal Cavity in the *Cyber-Augments* Menu
* Use *Ascension Cores* (AMP, NEO, FUSION Cores) to ascend weapons, armor, and abilities in the *Ascension Lab* Menu
* Abilities that have been upgraded to Rank 5 can be AMPED for *Tech Points*, *AMP Cores*, and *Credits* and then OVERLOADED for an even higher price, making them much stronger
* Tier 3 or 4 Weapons with 4/4 attachment slots filled can be ascended to NEO for *NEO Cores* and *credits*, and then OVERCLOCKED for an even higher price, making them much stronger (And unlocking a 5th attachment slot)
* Mk. IV or Higher Armor can be FUSED for *FUSION CORES* and *credits*, and then OVERSATURATED for an even higher price, making them stronger and unlocking OVERDRIVE for certain milestones
* Use credits to acquire consumable items, cosmetics, cores, augments, and parts in the *Supply Shop* menu
* Save your progress and read collected journal entries in the *Intel & Save* menu
* View achievement progress that rewards *credits* in the *Trophies* menu
* Finally, drop into missions in the *Sector Gate* Menu
* As a note, all menus can be accessed by their stations in the hub world
* Training dummies for testing weapons and abilities are also available
